WSPR Scheduler Live - what else this PC needs
=============================================

WSPR Scheduler Live shows who has heard your WSPR transmissions, from wspr.live, and saves the reports
in the WSPR Scheduler database (wspr_rx.reported) for WSPR Scheduler Map's TX map.

It needs:

1. MySQL or MariaDB with the WSPR Scheduler databases - the same ones WSPR Scheduler uses. If WSPR
   Scheduler already runs on this PC, nothing else is needed. Otherwise see "The WSPR Scheduler Database
   Configuration" at https://sourceforge.net/projects/wspr-scheduler/files/
   The database password and your callsign are read from DBcredential in your user folder (written by
   WSPR Scheduler). Without one, set your callsign with Config.

2. An internet connection (reports come from db1.wspr.live).

Don't run it together with the C# WSPR Scheduler Live: both would fetch the same reports.
